## 2. Does BookStitch use artificial intelligence?

Yes. Quite a lot, actually.

Artificial intelligence helped write parts of BookStitch, fix bugs, create icons, and occasionally introduce entirely new problems nobody had asked for.

BookStitch itself doesn't use artificial intelligence to process your audiobooks. It relies on traditional algorithms, FFmpeg, and a rather unreasonable amount of optimism.

The developer also claims to possess some intelligence of his own. This has not yet been independently verified.

Nevertheless, he insists he knows what he's doing.

The AI has declined to comment.

## 4. Yet another FFmpeg wrapper?

Yes, sir!

We wrap FFmpeg. Then we wrap the audio files. Then we wrap the wrapper around the files and the files around the wrapper.

Of course, a wrapper needs a proper wrapper, so we wrap that too.

Then we wrap everything in a user interface, wrap some metadata around it, and add another layer of wrapping, just in case.

At this point, nobody really knows what's inside anymore.

But it has more layers than a burrito.

And if something doesn't work, we just wrap it again.

Barely Operational Overengineered Kilobyte Stitching Technology Involving Terrible Coding Habits.
